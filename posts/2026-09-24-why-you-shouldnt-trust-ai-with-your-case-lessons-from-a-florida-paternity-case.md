Ryan v. Perez, 3D25-1254, September 23, 2026: The Third DCA affirmed a paternity judgment but rebuked the self-represented appellant, a Florida Bar member, for filing an initial brief with at least ten AI-hallucinated citations. The court denied his request for a "do over" corrected brief and warned that any future failure to verify citations will result in sanctions.
